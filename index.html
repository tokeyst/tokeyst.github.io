Research

Nonlinear waves and solitons

Solitary, multi-soliton, periodic and overtaking wave solutions of nonlinear evolution equations, and what happens when such waves meet.

This runs through all three published papers.

Plasma physics

Ion-acoustic waves in the upper ionosphere of Venus, in Cartesian, cylindrical and spherical geometry, and dust-acoustic waves in strongly coupled dusty plasma.

Three papers published in Scientific Reports and Advances in Space Research. A study of head-on soliton collisions in dusty plasma is in development.

Computational neuroscience

Fractional-order Hodgkin–Huxley neuron models with memristive and calcium-channel dynamics, and a fractal-fractional neuron-network model that merges Hodgkin–Huxley dynamics with Wilson–Cowan mean-field theory to describe neurodegenerative disease progression.

Current work at CUET. One paper is under submission.

Epidemiological modeling

A dengue transmission model that compares urban and rural areas of Bangladesh. Compartment models such as SIR describe how an outbreak rises, peaks and fades.

Presented at AMSE-2025 and submitted to its proceedings.

Research in motion

Two small interactive models, one from each side of my work. Both run in your browser.
