01.

About Me

I am a Research Assistant in Applied Mathematics with research experience in nonlinear dynamical systems, mathematical modeling, plasma physics, and computational neuroscience. My work has included nonlinear wave propagation, soliton interactions, fractional-order models, epidemiological modeling, and neuron dynamics.

I began research during my undergraduate studies through mathematical modeling of dengue transmission and later moved into plasma physics and nonlinear wave theory during my Master's. My current research focuses on mathematical models of neuronal dynamics and network behavior.
